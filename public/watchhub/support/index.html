WatchHub Support

WatchHub is a private iPhone app for discovering movies and television series and maintaining a personal watchlist.

Getting help

For technical assistance, authentication problems, privacy requests, or feedback, email bharat@prajana.ai.

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your WatchHub version and build number from Settings
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you were doing when the problem occurred
  • Whether you were online or offline
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if appropriate

Protect your information: Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive personal information.

Common questions

Which Google account should I use?

Use the Google account that has been authorized for the private WatchHub catalog.

Where is my watchlist stored?

Watchlist statuses and personal notes are stored locally on your iPhone in version 1.

Can I use my watchlist offline?

Yes. The watchlist remains available offline. Previously loaded catalog information may also remain available as saved content.

Does WatchHub stream or download video?

No. WatchHub provides catalog information and links to external destinations where available.

How do I remove my information?

Remove individual watchlist items inside the app or delete the app to remove its local data. For authentication access or privacy requests, contact bharat@prajana.ai.
