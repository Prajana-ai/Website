WatchHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

WatchHub is a private entertainment discovery and watchlist application operated by Prajana AI for its owner. This policy describes the information used by the application and its supporting service.

Information processed

  • Google Sign-In or Firebase email/password authentication provides an account identifier and authentication information so WatchHub can verify access to the private catalog. Depending on the selected provider and account, Google and Firebase may process a name, email address, phone number, device identifier, coarse location, and related authentication data.
  • Google Sign-In and Firebase may process limited usage and diagnostic information to provide, secure, and measure the authentication service. WatchHub does not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Watchlist status and personal notes are stored locally on the device in version 1. They are not uploaded to the WatchHub server.
  • The service receives ordinary network information, such as IP address, request time, and technical request metadata, through Google Cloud infrastructure logs for security and reliability.
  • WatchHub does not use advertising SDKs, sell personal information, or track activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

Catalog and external services

WatchHub displays catalog metadata and references obtained from sources including TMDB, YouTube, Apple, and watch providers. When the user chooses to play a trailer, WatchHub loads YouTube’s embedded player in a non-persistent system web view; that interaction and any external link are governed by the destination provider’s privacy policy. Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, and Google Cloud process information to authenticate authorized users and operate the private backend.

Retention and deletion

Local watchlist data remains on the device until an item is removed, the catalog cache is cleared where supported, or the app is deleted. Operational cloud logs are retained according to the configured Google Cloud retention policy. Authentication access can be revoked and deletion requests can be made using the contact below.

Security

The catalog database, images, credentials, and personal service state are stored in access-controlled Google Cloud resources. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but WatchHub uses encryption in transit and least-privilege access controls.

Children

WatchHub is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, access revocation, or deletion requests, contact bharat@prajana.ai.

This policy is publicly available at https://prajana.ai/watchhub/privacy and is linked from the WatchHub app.
