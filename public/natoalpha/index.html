    A few things, clearly answered.

    What is the NATO phonetic alphabet?

    It assigns a recognizable word to each letter: Alfa, Bravo, Charlie, and so on. Saying “B as in Bravo” is easier to distinguish than saying the letter B by itself over a call.

    Why TREE, FIFE and NINER?

    These are aviation pronunciations for three, five and nine. They help distinguish similar sounds over radio or a noisy connection. NATOAlpha shows both the familiar digit word and its pronunciation.

    Can I use it while calling on the same phone?

    Yes. Switch your call to speakerphone or headphones and open the reader. The mobile reading mode keeps the screen awake while the app is active. NATOAlpha is a visual aid; it doesn’t place calls, detect them, or send spoken audio into a call.

    Where can I download the app?

    The mobile apps are in development and aren’t listed in the stores yet. The live translator on this page is available now, with no installation.

    Does it read my clipboard or collect my code?

    This website only uses what you type or paste into its field, and translates it locally. It has no analytics or account system. The mobile apps save your most recent code locally; Clear removes that saved reading session.
